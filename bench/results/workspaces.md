## Reading the table

Row 4 and row 5 use a generated aggregate root: every solved member registered as a `"package"`-type repository (`dist.type: "path"`, since viv doesn't parse a top-level `path` repository — #305), required at `*` from a synthetic composer.json. Two gaps found while building it are filed rather than worked around: `self.version` in a member's own `require` fails to parse at all (#304, blocks every neos/neos-development-collection member that uses it — 24 of 29), and a member whose own composer.json declares a `path` repository (Sylius's every component, WooCommerce's main plugin) can't be solved at all, standalone or aggregate (#305). Both cut into row 4/5's coverage below, independent of whether a workspace would help.

**laravel/framework's row 4 is the standout number in the corpus: 54 of 58 comparable external requirements differ between a component's standalone solve and the aggregate.** Yet row 1 shows no evidence this repository's own members are installed alone (0 locks, 0 CI) — the dev repository components are read-only splits, tested and installed together via the monorepo, not solved individually here. The drift is real (each Illuminate component's looser standalone constraint lands on whatever is newest on Packagist today, where the aggregate's single solve settles every component on one shared version), but it's evidence for what a workspace would remove from a *published-split* consumer's install, not from this repository as it stands.

**acquia/acquia_cms and sylius/sylius show 0 members solved standalone at all**, for two different reasons. Every acquia_cms module conflicts tightly with specific version ranges of its siblings and of drupal/core, and several of those ranges are Packagist-flagged security advisories a solve won't offer by default — the distribution is designed to be installed as a whole, not decomposed. Sylius's failure is #305 alone: every component declares its own `path` repository to its siblings, which is exactly a workspace's own shape already, just one viv can't read yet.

**Row 5's warm numbers show the union-cost effect chapter 3 already expected from Composer today**, most sharply on typo3/typo3 (warm 2.845s independent vs 0.024s aggregate, ~118×) and woocommerce/woocommerce (0.243s vs 0.013s, ~19×): one shared `vendor/` costs the union once; N independent ones each pay to link it again even against the same store. Store size tells the same story from the other side — typo3 (53.3MB → 17.1MB) and newspack-workspace (134.2MB → 54.0MB) shrink under the aggregate, while apigee-edge-drupal, bagisto and shopware show no change at all, because those members share nothing external in the requirements this corpus could compare.

## Answering the question, in numbers

Row 1 gives evidence members are installed alone (a committed lock or a CI workflow installing a member on its own) on **3 of the 10 repositories**: automattic/newspack-workspace, woocommerce/woocommerce, bagisto/bagisto. Of those 3, row 4 shows **0** with measurable drift on the external packages they could compare (8, 8 and 6 comparisons respectively — every member solved standalone happened to already agree with the aggregate on its own external requirements). The one repository with large, unambiguous row-4 drift, laravel/framework, has no row-1 evidence its members are installed alone *as this repository is used today* — the drift there is real for the published-split components this repository sources, not for the dev monorepo itself.
